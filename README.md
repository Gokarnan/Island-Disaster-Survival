# 🌪️ Island Disaster Survival

**Island Disaster Survival** is a mobile-friendly HTML5 survival game where you and a group of AI bots are dropped onto an island and must survive a random natural disaster. When the disaster ends, anyone still alive wins—and if more than one player survives, everyone receives the same coin reward.

Survive disasters, earn coins, unlock cosmetics, and become the last one standing!

---

## 📱 How to Play

The game is designed for **mobile devices** and uses touch controls.

### Movement

- Touch and hold the **left side of the screen** to bring up the virtual joystick.
- Drag the joystick in any direction to move your character.
- The farther you drag the joystick, the faster you move.
- Release the joystick to stop moving.

### Buttons

- **Jump:** Press the jump button to jump over gaps, climb onto objects, or escape rising hazards.
- **Sprint:** Use sprint to move faster when escaping a disaster.
- **Interact:** Use this button when near interactive objects, if available.
- **Emote:** Use emotes to celebrate, panic, or communicate with other survivors.

You can adjust the joystick’s **size, opacity, dead zone, and position** in the Settings menu.

---

## 🎮 Game Rounds

Each round follows the same basic flow:

1. **Lobby**
   - You and the AI bots wait in a safe area before the round begins.

2. **Island Teleport**
   - Everyone is transported to a randomly generated island.

3. **Preparation Phase**
   - You have a short amount of time to explore the island.
   - Look for high ground, shelter, sturdy buildings, and escape routes.

4. **Disaster Phase**
   - A random natural disaster begins.
   - Survive until the timer runs out.

5. **Round Results**
   - Survivors are announced.
   - Coins are awarded.
   - The game returns to the lobby for the next round.

---

## 🌊 Disasters

Every round features a different disaster. Each one requires a different survival strategy.

| Disaster | How to Survive |
|---|---|
| **Tsunami** | Get to high ground before the wave floods the island. |
| **Tornado** | Stay away from its path and avoid being pulled into the air. |
| **Earthquake** | Avoid unstable buildings and collapsing platforms. |
| **Meteor Shower** | Watch for falling meteors and move away from impact zones. |
| **Lightning Storm** | Avoid tall objects and open areas during strikes. |
| **Wildfire** | Move away from spreading flames and burning structures. |
| **Acid Rain** | Stay under a roof or shelter to avoid taking damage. |
| **Blizzard** | Find shelter to avoid freezing and reduced visibility. |

Pay attention to the warning messages, sky changes, sounds, and countdown timer before each disaster begins.

---

## 💀 If You Die

Dying does not end your session.

When you die, you enter **Spectator Mode**. You can watch the remaining survivors and bots until the round ends.

In spectator mode, you can:

- Drag around the screen to rotate the camera.
- Pinch to zoom in and out.
- Follow a specific survivor.
- Use free camera mode to explore the island.

You will automatically be revived when the next round begins.

---

## 🏆 Winning and Rewards

There are two ways a round can end:

- **Last Survivor Wins:** If only one player or bot remains, they win immediately.
- **Time Runs Out:** If multiple survivors are still alive when the disaster timer ends, they all win together.

### Coin Rewards

| Result | Reward |
|---|---|
| Survive the disaster | **25 coins** |
| Be the last survivor | **+50 bonus coins** |
| Die but stay until the round ends | **5 participation coins** |

If several players survive, they all receive the **same survivor reward**

## 🪙 Coin Shop

Coins can be spent in the **Shop** from the lobby.

The shop contains cosmetic items only. Cosmetics do not affect gameplay or survival chances.

### Cosmetic Types

- **Character skins**
- **Hats and head accessories**
- **Faces and masks**
- **Movement trails**
- **Victory effects**
- **Emotes**

Items come in different rarities:

- Common
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary

You can buy items, equip them, preview them on your character, and randomize your outfit. Your coins and equipped cosmetics are saved automatically.

---

## 💾 Saving Progress

Your progress is saved in your browser using `localStorage`.

This includes:

- Your coin balance
- Cosmetics you own
- Cosmetics you have equipped
- Joystick and control settings

Clearing your browser data may reset your progress.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open the game in a mobile browser.
2. Complete the short controls tutorial.
3. Adjust your joystick settings if needed.
4. Enter a round from the lobby.
5. Explore the island during the preparation phase.
6. Survive the disaster.
7. Earn coins and unlock cosmetics.

Good luck—can you become the last survivor?
